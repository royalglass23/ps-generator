# AI system selection images

Generated on 2026-10-06 for the PS Generator system-selection interface.

## Usage and provenance

- These images are original AI-generated compositions created from structured prompts.
- No Royal Glass, Juralco, UNEX, Viking, EDGE, or other supplier photograph was copied directly into the set. User-supplied canopy photos were used to communicate support-arm, frame, slat, and glass geometry. The glass-canopy asset is an AI-enhanced derivative of the supplied photo and should be published only if Royal Glass owns it or has permission to adapt it.
- No supplier logo, product mark, readable label, or watermark was requested.
- The images are illustrative product-family guides, not engineering drawings, installation instructions, compliance evidence, or verified depictions of a particular proprietary profile.
- Product names in filenames are internal mapping labels. They do not imply supplier endorsement.
- Before public release, Royal Glass should visually and technically review every image. Copyright risk from copied photography has been reduced by generating new compositions and limiting the canopy reference to generic fixing geometry, but zero infringement risk cannot be guaranteed; exact product appearance may also be protected by design rights.

## Visual direction

All assets use a square close-up composition, foreground hardware, clear application context, contemporary New Zealand architecture, natural daylight, no people, and no text or branding. Backgrounds deliberately avoid the Sky Tower and other recognisable CBD landmarks.

## Prompt framework

Each prompt required an original square photorealistic architectural composition, with no reproduction or imitation of a supplier photograph, no logos or brand names, no readable text or watermark, and no people. System-specific prompt clauses described only the generic visible fixing family, mounting orientation, material, and application context listed in the asset map above.

The primary canopy image shows an original open aluminium canopy composition with a perimeter frame, repeated narrow battens, and wall-mounted cantilever arms. The secondary image is an enhanced square re-render of a user-supplied glass-canopy photo: its perspective, exposure, facade, clutter, glass clarity, and metal finish were improved while retaining the photographed canopy construction. The remaining assets were precisely edited to replace recognisable Auckland skyline elements with generic New Zealand suburban hills, harbour, or sky while preserving the system hardware.
