# PS1 Process Questions for Roxy

These questions are intentionally unresolved. The prototype must not present them as confirmed Royal Glass operating policy until Roxy answers them.

## Application acknowledgement and engagement timing

Does submitting a PS1 application only confirm that the supplied information is accurate and authorize Royal Glass to review it?

If the review identifies paid engineering or other chargeable work, does Royal Glass then issue a separate scope, price and engagement for approval before that work begins?

## Technical correspondence

When the applicant and nominated technical contact are different people, who should receive:

- requests for missing or further information;
- technical and design questions;
- pricing or engagement documents;
- the completed PS1; and
- general application status updates?

In particular, should the nominated technical contact be the primary recipient while the applicant is copied, or should the applicant control the recipients for each category?

## Initial need and final request

Roxy's P1 logic asks at the beginning whether the applicant needs a PS1, needs a quotation first, or is unsure. Step 7 later asks the applicant to choose Price First, Proceed with PS1, or Not Sure.

Are these intended to be two distinct decisions—an initial reason for visiting and a final instruction after supplying the project details—or should the journey ask this only once?
